## Executive Summary

This document outlines the 12-week development roadmap for BabyTrack Flutter, organized into 4 phases. Each phase builds on the previous one to deliver incremental value.

| Auto-detection rules | Cloud Function | P1 |

**Auto-Detection Rules**:
```dart
// Sleep milestones
'first_6hr_sleep' → sleepDuration >= 360 minutes
'first_8hr_sleep' → sleepDuration >= 480 minutes
'sleep_score_90' → sleepScore >= 90 (first time)

// Health milestones
'stable_temperature' → 7 days in normal range
'consistent_vitals' → Low variance for 7 days
```

| Risk | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|--------|------------|

| Subscription integration | Revenue delay | Early RevenueCat setup |
