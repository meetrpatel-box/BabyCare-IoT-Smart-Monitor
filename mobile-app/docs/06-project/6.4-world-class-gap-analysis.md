**Document ID**: DOC-6.4

## Executive Summary

This document analyzes BabyTrack against **world-class baby monitoring apps** (Nanit, Owlet, Huckleberry, Baby Connect, Glow Baby) and identifies **critical infrastructure gaps** that prevent us from delivering a production-grade application.

**Current State**: The app has solid foundations but is **missing 40%+ of what's needed** for world-class quality.
